\section{Profile}

Earth-observation scientist with a PhD and 8+ peer-reviewed publications applying machine learning to satellite imagery for agriculture. Builds end-to-end pipelines on Sentinel-1/-2 and Landsat data — from ingestion and feature engineering to classification and validation — to answer real questions about crops, water and drought.
