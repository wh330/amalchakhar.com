\section{Profile}

Builds end-to-end pipelines — from ingestion and feature engineering to classification, validation and policy-ready maps — to answer real questions about crops, water and drought.

\textbf{Doctoral Researcher}, University of Castilla-La Mancha -- Albacete, Spain \hfill 2019 -- 2023
\vspace{0.05cm}
\begin{highlights}
    \item Benchmarked 22 non-parametric classifiers across 12 crop types and 2{,}000+ field plots in semi-arid Spain, fusing Landsat-8 and Sentinel-2 to reach \textbf{$\sim$90\% F1} (\emph{Remote Sensing}, 2020).
    \item Boosted crop-classification accuracy by integrating Sentinel-1 SAR (VV/VH) with Sentinel-2 NDVI across 9 feature scenarios, identifying the optimal feature--classifier pairing (\emph{Remote Sensing}, 2021).
    \item Detected irrigated vs.\ rainfed orchards at \textbf{88.4\% accuracy} from Sentinel-1/-2 time series, and built an automated Sentinel Hub download-and-tiling pipeline (\emph{Remote Sensing}, 2024).
    \item Retrieved surface soil moisture (\textbf{R\textsuperscript{2} up to 0.78}) by inverting IEM+WCM scattering models with neural networks, using UAV photogrammetry to measure surface roughness (\emph{Remote Sensing}, 2021).
\end{highlights}
